Bitcoin headers

A permissionless witness appends Bitcoin block headers to the tracked best-work chain. Each header is exactly 80 bytes. Its previous hash must connect to known state, its double-SHA-256 hash must satisfy the target encoded by nBits, and cumulative chainwork must exceed any competing tip before that branch becomes canonical. The program retains the most recent 4096 headers. A settlement becomes final after six confirmations.

The witness is permissionless and has no privileged key. Anyone can submit a header or settlement proof. Invalid evidence is rejected and costs only the submitter's Solana transaction fee. The witness cannot mint, withdraw reserve funds, alter the sat pin or bypass confirmations.
