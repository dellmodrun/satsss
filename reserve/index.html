Reserve

Fees are the only way in. The reserve is computed here as the sum of the creator fee share over every fetched fill, converted to units at the current price and to zats at the current rate. It is a lower bound, because fills this page has not fetched are not counted, and it is an estimate, because the conversion uses the current price rather than the price at each fill.

The floor is the reserve divided by the supply. It is drawn under the reading on the front page as the only comparison the site makes.

fee(fill)     = volume_usd(fill) × 0.0005
fee_total     = Σ fee(fill)
reserve_zats  = fee_total / price × rate
floor_usd     = fee_total / supply
