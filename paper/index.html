sats: A Supply Pinned to One Hundred Million Sats

Abstract: I am a Solana supply with a fixed Bitcoin denomination. One BTC contains one hundred million sats, and exactly one hundred million sats are pinned to me. My token supply may shrink but can never expand. Every fill is quantized into whole sats and the remainder is burned. A Bitcoin transaction can publish a versioned SATS commitment in an OP_RETURN output. A permissionless relayer submits the transaction, its Merkle branch and the corresponding Bitcoin header chain to Solana. After proof of work, confirmation and replay checks pass, the program applies the committed settlement atomically. I am not wrapped BTC and I do not custody bitcoin. I am a verifiable link between Bitcoin settlement evidence and a one-way Solana supply rule. This page exposes the pin, dust, reserve, Bitcoin headers, settlement receipts and every assumption needed to audit the mechanism. The reading in the margin is current.

The pin

A sat is one hundred millionth of one BTC. The program borrows Bitcoin's atomic denomination without issuing wrapped bitcoin or claiming custody of BTC. At initialization it records 100000000 in the state account as the immutable sat pin. The mint's circulating supply represents exactly that denomination at genesis, and sats per token are always the pin divided by circulating supply. Because mint authority is removed and the supply can only fall, sats represented by each remaining unit can only rise.

Dust

When a fill moves tokens, the program computes the represented sat amount at the current rate and rounds down to whole sats. The remainder is dust. Dust is never rounded up or carried forward: the equivalent token fraction is burned. Each accepted fill therefore resolves to an integer sat denomination while circulating supply moves in only one direction.

The reserve

Creator fees accrue to the Solana reserve account. The interface converts the observed fee value into token units at the current market price and then into sats at the enforced rate. Reserve value divided by circulating supply produces the displayed floor. This market-derived estimate is kept separate from Bitcoin proof verification: a price feed cannot create a settlement and a settlement proof cannot set the market price.

The settlement commitment

A Bitcoin settlement transaction carries one provably unspendable OP_RETURN output containing a 32-byte SHA-256 commitment. Its preimage binds the SATS protocol tag, version, action, sat weight, Solana mint and a unique nonce. A relayer submits the raw transaction, preimage, transaction index, Merkle branch and referenced Bitcoin header. The Solana program recomputes the commitment and Merkle root before any state transition.

The header chain

The program tracks Bitcoin's best-work header chain through permissionless relayers. Each 80-byte header must link to the accepted chain and satisfy its encoded proof-of-work target. A settlement remains pending until its transaction Merkle branch matches the header root and six additional headers confirm it. The relayer supplies evidence but receives no authority: invalid headers, branches, commitments and duplicate outpoints are rejected deterministically.

The settlement tree

Every accepted fill or Bitcoin settlement appends a receipt leaf to the program's commitment tree. A Bitcoin receipt binds txid, output index, block hash, sat weight, action and nonce. A replay marker derived from the outpoint and nonce can be consumed only once. The root is a compact audit record while the replay set prevents the same Bitcoin evidence from affecting Solana state twice.

What is claimed

Nothing here is a claim about value. The claim is that a denomination can be enforced, that a supply can be bound to a number and only move away from it in one direction, and that every part of the binding can be verified from outside by reading accounts that anyone can read.
